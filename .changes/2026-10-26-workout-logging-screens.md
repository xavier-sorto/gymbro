GymBro now has its workout screens: start a Push, Pull, Legs, Chest & Back or Shoulders & Arms day, log sets with one tap, see last time, best and today's target, add your own exercises, get a rest timer with a beep, and finish the workout with a summary.

You can also edit your day types from Home: add exercises from the library or type a new one, remove or reorder them, star main lifts and rename the day.
